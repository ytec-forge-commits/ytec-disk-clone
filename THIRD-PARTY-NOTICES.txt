Y-TEC Tsumugi Drive - Third-Party Notices
===========================================

The Y-TEC product payload bundles the LINE Seed JP font data and statically
links Zstandard and Argon2 as described below. The Portable ZIP does not
redistribute third-party executables or DLLs, Microsoft WIM/ISO/CAB files, or
Windows ADK / Windows PE content. Rescue media are created locally from the
user's installed and verified Microsoft ADK / Windows PE components.
