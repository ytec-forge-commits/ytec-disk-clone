# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Y-TEC Tsumugi Drive contributors, maintainers, and participants commit to a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We also commit to acting responsibly around safety-critical software. People
reporting a mistake, near miss, or safety concern must be treated constructively
and must not be blamed for raising it in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- State uncertainty and validation limits honestly.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and repair harm.
- Protect private information and follow the project's safe-reporting rules.
- Prioritize the safety of users and their data over speed or reputation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, sexualized attention, or
  deliberate intimidation.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or knowingly false claims about contributors
  or project results.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to expose credentials, disk images, recovery keys,
  identifying logs, or other sensitive material.
- Encouraging unsafe testing on production systems or real data.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a conduct, security, or safety issue.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, Issues, Pull Requests, reviews,
discussions, release coordination, and public spaces where someone is
representing the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a security vulnerability, use the repository Security tab's Private
Vulnerability Reporting option. For a private conduct or community matter, use
the repository's `Private contact request` Issue Form and include no incident
details. A maintainer will arrange a private channel when possible.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality as far as
practical, and avoid conflicts of interest. Proportionate responses may include
a private correction, warning, temporary participation restriction, removal of
content, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. A maintainer named in a
report must not make the final enforcement decision for that report.

Good-faith safety or security reporting is protected even when the report is
ultimately not reproducible.
